Workplace

Corporate transport in a hybrid workplace

Plan for changing attendance without losing predictability.

Buus Editorial · 3 min read

Make demand visible

Hybrid schedules change the number of passengers from day to day. Bring together planned office attendance, shift requirements and transport bookings so teams can see where demand is concentrated.

Define clear booking rules

Explain when passengers should book, how to cancel and where to check pickup details. A consistent process helps employees plan their day and gives the operation a clearer view of expected demand.

Review service patterns

Compare demand across weekdays and locations. Use recurring patterns to guide the base schedule, and review exceptions with the teams responsible for workplace planning.
