Workplace

A better journey begins before boarding

Clear information and accessible support matter throughout the commute.

Buus Editorial · 3 min read

Communicate the essentials

Make departure times, pickup locations and route updates easy to find. Give passengers a consistent place to check the latest information before they travel.

Support everyday exceptions

Employees may change shifts, miss a departure or need help locating a stop. Define a support process that directs each request to the right team and explains what the passenger can expect next.

Close the feedback loop

Collect feedback around specific stages of the journey. Review recurring issues, communicate relevant changes and check whether those changes improved the experience.
